Version: 0.4.0
Date: 26. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Added Portal Pipe (portal-pipe-to-ground) for transporting fluids between any two points, even across planets. Crafted from 4 vanilla underground pipes.
    - Paired portal pipes equalize their fluid bidirectionally every 2 ticks; temperature is blended by volume, and two different fluids never mix.
    - Portal pipes share the complete pairing workflow with belts: candidate list, live preview, map jump and unlock.
  Gui:
    - The partner list now shows all unpaired entities of the same kind. Pairable ones get a Select button; belts of the same form show a "press R to flip" hint instead of being hidden.
    - The list header now states the current form and which form is required.
    - Hovering the mouse over a belt or pipe registers it immediately, as a safety net for any missed build event.
  Bugfixes:
    - Pairing validation now rejects cross-kind pairs (belt with pipe).

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 26. 10. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Newly placed belts are registered immediately again: on_built_entity and on_robot_built_entity use the entity field, which was renamed in Factorio 2.0 (reading the old created_entity silently failed).
    - Entities built or revived by scripts are registered as well (script_raised_built / script_raised_revive).
  Interface:
    - /list-portals output no longer starts with a numeric ID prefix.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 26. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Reworked pairing: pick a partner from a list of actual unpaired belts instead of typing matching names.
    - A paired belt is locked: its window shows a live preview, the partner's coordinates and a single Unlock button; unlocking returns both belts to the unpaired state.
    - Belt names are now optional notes and no longer take part in pairing; the "name exists" and "same direction" errors are gone because invalid states can no longer be selected.
  Gui:
    - Selecting a candidate pairs instantly and switches the window to the locked view; entries are re-validated at click time in case they were paired, removed or flipped while the window was open.
